Does TJ Maxx do price adjustments?

By Tag Hunter · Based on the Clearance Hunter Handbook · Updated 25 September 2026

No. According to TJ Maxx's published returns information, it doesn't offer price adjustments on marked-down or clearance items you have already bought. If you buy a coat and it carries a lower red sticker a week later, the difference isn't refunded.

Worth double-checking

Store policies get updated. This reflects TJ Maxx's stated US policy as checked in September 2026. Confirm anything that matters on TJ Maxx's official returns page before you rely on it.

What you can do instead

If you are still inside the return window and you have the receipt, you can return the item and buy it again at the new price. That gets you the same result as a price adjustment would have.

Before you return anything, check that the lower-priced one is still in stock. If it has sold, you have returned the item for nothing.

Keep the receipt

The receipt is the difference between a refund and a store credit. Clearance purchases are exactly the ones you are most likely to reconsider, so photograph the receipt when you get to the car.

Why this changes how you shop

Because a later drop isn't refunded, the decision to buy now or wait matters at the shelf. A red sticker means the item is likely to fall again if it doesn't sell. Yellow is the final markdown. Our tag colors guide covers how to read each one, and the markdown schedule guide covers timing.

What is confirmed and what isn't

This guide is written for TJ Maxx stores in the United States and summarizes stated policy, not what any single store does. Individual stores and associates can differ, so treat it as orientation.

Questions

Does TJ Maxx do price adjustments?

According to TJ Maxx's published returns information, it does not offer price adjustments on marked-down or clearance merchandise you already bought. If the item carries a lower price a week later, the difference is not refunded.

If an item I bought goes on clearance, can I get the difference back?

No, not as a price adjustment. What you can do instead, if you are still inside the return window and have your receipt, is return the item and buy it again at the new price.

Can I return a clearance item and buy it again cheaper?

The handbook describes it as a way to get the same result as a price adjustment, as long as you are inside the return window and still have the receipt. Check that the lower-priced item is still in stock before you return yours.

Does the receipt matter when returning something to TJ Maxx?

Yes. The receipt is often the difference between a refund and a store credit, so keep it, and a photo of it works, until you are sure you are keeping the item.

How can I avoid buying something right before it drops?

You can't always avoid it, but you can read the sticker. Red means the item is likely to fall again if it doesn't sell, while yellow is the final markdown. Timing is local to each store, so watching your own store helps.
