Does TJ Maxx have Black Friday sales?

By Tag Hunter · Based on the Clearance Hunter Handbook and TJ Maxx's return policy pages · Updated 30 October 2026

We found no official TJ Maxx Black Friday sale, and shoppers reportedly say there isn't a doorbuster event. The store already prices below department stores every day, and its real savings come from markdown stickers. Here is what that means for November, plus TJ Maxx's official 2026 holiday return dates.

Black Friday at TJ Maxx

TJX does not announce a Black Friday sale for TJ Maxx that we could find, and shoppers reportedly say the store skips the one-day doorbuster model. That fits how off-price works: the shelf prices are meant to be low all year, so there is no big markdown event to build a sale day around. Store hours around the holiday vary, so check your own store's page rather than trusting a number from a blog.

What to do instead

  • Read the stickers, not the calendar. Red stickers appear all year, and a day that isn't a sale day can still be a good day to find a marked-down item.
  • Gift shop with the tag in mind. A tag tells you if you are paying full ticket or a markdown. Our tag colors guide covers what each one means.
  • Save the big hunt for later. The deepest clearance comes in January. See when TJ Maxx has clearance sales.

2026 holiday return dates, per TJ Maxx

TJ Maxx's return policy pages (checked 30 October 2026) list an extended holiday window for gifts:

  • In-store purchases made between 10/4/2026 and 12/24/2026 may be returned through 1/25/2027.
  • Online purchases made in the same window may be returned through 2/4/2027.
  • From 12/26/2026 the normal return policy applies to new purchases.
  • Gift returns with a receipt receive a store credit, not a refund to the original payment.

The standard rules, which apply outside the holiday window, are 30 days in store with a valid register receipt, and 40 days from the order date for online purchases unless your shipping confirmation email says otherwise. Online orders can be returned in a store, and items bought in a TJ Maxx store can only be returned to a TJ Maxx store, not Marshalls or other sister brands. Dates can change, so confirm on TJ Maxx's own return policy page before you rely on them.

After Christmas

Shoppers report holiday merchandise being marked down once Christmas passes, with the deepest clearance arriving in January. TJX doesn't publish these dates, and stores differ, so watch your own. Our yellow sticker guide explains what final clearance means.

Two catches worth knowing

Holiday timing has a trap on both sides: buying a gift just before it drops, and returning one outside its window. The handbook covers the day-after rule and the month-by-month clearance calendar (page 17) and the full returns table (page 24).

What is confirmed and what isn't

The return dates and rules above are from TJ Maxx's own return policy pages as of 30 October 2026. The rest, including Black Friday, comes from our own searches and shopper reports: we found no official TJ Maxx Black Friday announcement, but absence of one is not proof. TJX has never published its internal markdown schedule. This guide is written for TJ Maxx stores in the United States. Marshalls and HomeGoods have their own policies and timelines.

Questions

Does TJ Maxx have Black Friday sales?

We found no official TJ Maxx Black Friday sale announcement, and shoppers reportedly say the store does not run doorbuster-style Black Friday events. Prices are already marked below department store prices every day, so the better plan is to watch for markdown stickers.

What is TJ Maxx's holiday return policy for 2026?

According to TJ Maxx's return policy pages, in-store purchases made between 10/4/2026 and 12/24/2026 may be returned through 1/25/2027, and online purchases made in the same window may be returned through 2/4/2027. The normal policy applies to purchases made from 12/26/2026.

What is the normal TJ Maxx return window?

According to TJ Maxx's return policy pages, in-store purchases can be returned within 30 days with a valid register receipt for a refund, and online purchases within 40 days of the order date unless the shipping confirmation email says otherwise.

Can I return a TJ Maxx gift for cash?

According to TJ Maxx's return policy pages, gift returns with a receipt receive a store credit, not a refund to the original payment.

When does TJ Maxx start after-Christmas clearance?

TJX does not publish its markdown dates. Shoppers report holiday merchandise being marked down after Christmas, with the deepest clearance coming in January. The timing differs by store, so watch your own.
