08 FAQ

Questions

Does it work at Marshalls and HomeGoods?

Marshalls and HomeGoods are sister stores of TJ Maxx, and shoppers report similar tag systems across all three. The book is built around TJ Maxx. Where we couldn't confirm that something holds at the other stores, it says so.

Is this US only?

Yes. The book covers TJ Maxx, Marshalls and HomeGoods stores in the United States. Stores in other countries may run things differently, and the book doesn't cover them.

Isn't this all free on TikTok?

Pieces of it are, scattered across videos, mixed with rumours, and with no way to tell which is which. The handbook puts it in one 28-page PDF, in order, and labels what we couldn't confirm. Try the free Spotter Card below and judge for yourself.

Will it go out of date?

Stores can change how they do things. That's why the book separates confirmed rules from rumours, and includes an observation sheet so you can check what your own store does.

What format is it? Can I print it?

A 28-page PDF. It opens on any phone, tablet or computer. Print it if you like, or keep it on your phone for the store.

What happens right after I pay?

Checkout confirms your order and sends you to a confirmation page. Your receipt is emailed right away with a Download button, so you can always get the file again from that email.

Do I need an account?

No. Pay with a card, PayPal, Apple Pay or Google Pay and download. You only need an email address for your receipt and download link.
